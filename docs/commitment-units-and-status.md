# Units and disclosure status define what can be aggregated

Method update: October 18, 2026. Proposed experiments below are not reported as executed results.

The snapshot contains quantities such as accelerator systems, individual accelerators and custom wafer starts. Sorting them is a display choice; summing them into one volume is dimensionally meaningless. A disclosed commitment also differs from a delivered shipment, an allocated packaging slot or an inferred production estimate.

The implemented confidence gate now rejects nonfinite, boolean and out-of-range bounds in addition to inverted intervals. Those checks make numeric output well-formed; they do not empirically calibrate the parser's confidence bands. Retain the disclosed/inferred flag and source anchor independently of those bands.

The next proposed fixture uses one commitment that appears in two filings and is later amended. Identify the economic commitment before deciding whether to update, supplement or create another edge. Aggregate quantities only within compatible units and statuses, with an explicit conversion source if a conversion is needed. A timestamped transcript sentence supports what it says at that time; it does not by itself establish fulfillment. Keep the old snapshot when new evidence changes the interpretation.
